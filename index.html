SPARTAN RUN — First-Person

Halo-inspired FPS rogue-lite prototype. Click to lock the mouse (Esc pauses and opens settings). If your browser blocks pointer lock, hold the mouse button and drag to look.

5x
Click to lock the mouse and look around
WASD move
Mouse look / hold to fire
Q grenade
Space jump
F melee
Shift + A/D thruster dash
Shift + W sprint (versus)
R reload
1-3 pick boon
M / N mute all / music
Right-click zoom
Wheel / 1-2 swap weapon
Esc / P pause & settings
Tab multiplayer scores
